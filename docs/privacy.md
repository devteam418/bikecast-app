# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 5, 2026*

This policy explains how the BikeCast iPhone app and its widget handle your data.

## In short

BikeCast has no user accounts and no BikeCast servers. It contains no advertising or analytics tools, and we don't sell your data or track you. To work, the app contacts weather and bike-share services directly from your iPhone.

## Data stored on your iPhone

BikeCast keeps the following on your iPhone:

- your bike network, stations, departure times, walking and cycling times, and preferences
- recent forecasts and station data, so the app and widget stay useful when you're offline

We can't access this data. Deleting the app removes it.

BikeCast asks for your stations rather than your home or work address, but your usual stations can suggest where you live or work.

## Location

Location access is optional and only used while the app is in use. BikeCast uses your location to:

- choose the right trip and find nearby stations
- suggest nearby networks
- estimate walking time, which is calculated on your iPhone
- get the forecast for where you are, if you use your own bike

You can change this at any time in **iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services › BikeCast**. Without location access, you can still choose a network and stations by hand, but the forecast for your own bike won't be available.

## Services BikeCast contacts

| Service | What it receives | Why |
|---|---|---|
| [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/en/terms) | Coordinates of your stations and their midpoint or, with your own bike, your current location | Weather forecasts |
| Your selected bike network or its data provider | A request for its public station data, without your location | Stations and availability |
| [Apple](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) (Maps) | Requests needed to display maps | Station maps |
| [Apple](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) (Weather) | The forecast location and place name, only when you tap a forecast | Opening the Apple Weather app |

These services can also receive technical data such as your IP address, and their own privacy policies apply. If you've agreed to share diagnostics with app developers, Apple may also provide us with crash reports and usage statistics.

## This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses no analytics or advertising cookies. GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses, as described in its [privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). If you contact us through GitHub, your request and username are public and subject to GitHub's terms.

## Your choices and rights

You can change your settings, turn off location access or delete the app at any time. Because we don't hold your data on our own servers, there's usually nothing for us to access or delete. Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal data, including access, correction, deletion and objection, and you can complain to your data protection authority. In France, that's the [CNIL](https://www.cnil.fr/).

## Children

BikeCast isn't intended specifically for children, and we don't knowingly collect children's personal data.

## Changes

We'll publish any change to this policy on this page and update the date above.

## Contact

For privacy questions, [open a request on GitHub](https://github.com/devteam418/bikecast-app/issues/new). Requests are public, so don't include personal details.
