# BikeCast support

## Contact us

To report a problem or ask a question, [open a request on GitHub](https://github.com/devteam418/bikecast-app/issues/new). You'll need a free GitHub account.

Please include your iPhone model, your iOS version, and your city and bike network. Requests are public, so don't share your home address or other personal details.

## Getting started

1. Open **Settings** with the gear button.
2. Under **City & bike network**, choose your network, or turn on **I use my own bike**.
3. Under **Usual stations**, choose your home and work stations.
4. Set your departure times, walking times and cycling time, then tap **Save**.

## Common questions

### Why is no weather showing?

With a bike-share network, BikeCast only shows the forecast for your route. Check that both stations are selected and belong to your current network. With your own bike, BikeCast needs your location: allow it in **iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services › BikeCast**.

### Why do the numbers differ from what I see at the station?

The numbers come from the live feed published by each network, which can lag behind the station. They show availability right now and don't predict what will be available when you leave, so check again before heading out.

### What do the green, orange and red signals mean?

Green means weather, bikes and docks all look good. Orange means something is less favorable, such as rain, wind or few bikes. Red means heavy rain, freezing rain, a storm or no free docks at your arrival station. The reasons appear next to the signal.

### How do I remove the extra forecast columns?

Go to **Settings › Weather window** and set **Padding** to 0 minutes. Only departure, mid-route and arrival remain.

### My network isn't listed. What can I do?

In the city list, choose **Custom URL...** and enter your network's GBFS address, if it publishes one. You can also [ask us to add it](https://github.com/devteam418/bikecast-app/issues/new).

BikeCast shows what each network publishes. If a feed reports no bikes, or is temporarily down, BikeCast can't show more. Tell us if the problem persists.

### How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap **Edit › Add Widget**, then choose BikeCast. To change what it shows, touch and hold the widget and tap **Edit Widget**. iOS decides how often widgets refresh, so opening the app gives you the latest data.

### How do I change the language or colors?

Language is in **Settings**. Colors are behind the palette button at the top of the main screen.

## Your data and location

- **No account:** BikeCast doesn't ask you to sign up and contains no advertising or analytics tools.
- **Your profile:** your stations, times and preferences are stored on your iPhone.
- **Location:** BikeCast uses it to choose the right trip, find nearby stations and estimate walking time on your iPhone.
- **Weather:** BikeCast sends coordinates to [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/) to get forecasts. These are your stations and their midpoint with a bike-share network, and your current location with your own bike.
- **Bike data:** BikeCast downloads station data directly from your selected network. Those requests don't include your location.

Open-Meteo and bike networks can receive technical data such as your IP address when your iPhone contacts them.
